## Safety rules (same as this project's rules)

- Never pay to apply for any job, on any platform above.
- Prefer platforms that show company career pages / confirm worldwide eligibility (Remote4Africa, TrulyRemoteWork, Virtual Click Jobs, Himalayas, Dynamite Jobs).
- Freelance platforms are income marketplaces — watch for survey/gig scams there.
- Ignore any job asking for bank details or "registration/processing fees."
