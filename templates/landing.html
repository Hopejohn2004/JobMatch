FAQ

The things everyone asks

Will the AI make up things about me?

No. Your dates, employers, qualifications and numbers are locked. The AI only reorders and rewords what is already in your CV, and any claim it cannot source from your original gets dropped.

Where does my CV actually live?

On the machine running JobMatch. Uploading it here sends it to that machine, not to a third-party CV store. Delete the file and it is gone.

Which job sites are searched?

RemoteOK, We Work Remotely, Arbeitnow, Jobgether, Remote.co, Himalayas and a handful of Nigerian boards, plus company career pages via the crawler. New sources get added without you doing anything.

Do I need a phone or a laptop?

Either. JobMatch runs on your own hardware and the dashboard is designed for the browser on your phone — same Wi-Fi or an optional tunnel.

What happens after the free trial?

You pick ₦1,500/month, or you do not. Nothing auto-renews without you choosing it, and your tailored CVs stay on disk either way.
